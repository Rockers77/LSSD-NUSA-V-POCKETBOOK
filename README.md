# LSSD Pocketbook — Los Santos County Sheriff's Department (2026 Edition)

Panduan saku digital (*Field Pocketbook*) berbasis web statis untuk personel **Los Santos County Sheriff's Department (LSSD)**. Dirancang ringan, responsif, dan mendukung navigasi tautan langsung (*hash routing*) seperti `#chain-of-command` untuk kebutuhan referensi cepat di lapangan.

---

## Dokumen Referensi Resmi

Seluruh konten, kode radio, struktur komando, dan standar operasional pada situs ini disusun berdasarkan:

1. **LSSD Communication & Procedural Reference Guide (`LSSD-DOC-SOP-00126`)** — Volume I, Edisi 2026
   - Section 100: Radio Unit Designations & Chain of Command (`SHERIFF 00` – `10`, `ARCHER 01` – `02`)
   - Section 101: Unit Callsigns (Patrol, Specialized, SEB, Detective, Aviation)
   - Section 102: Standard 10-Codes Directory
   - Section 103: Response & Operational Status Codes (`Code 0`, `Code 2`, `Code 3`, `Code 4`)
   - Section 104: Miranda Warning (English & Local Version)
   - Section 105–106: Radio Communication Standard, Discipline & Policy Compliance

2. **LSSD Patrol Operations Handbook (`LSSD-DOC-SOP-00226`)** — Division: Patrol Operations, Edisi 2026
   - `2-06/000.00 – 020.00`: Patrol Functions, Responsibilities & Jurisdiction
   - `2-06/030.00 – 030.20`: Scene Management, Traffic Stop, Pursuit, Detective Referral & MDT Reports
   - `2-06/030.25`: Patrol Priority Response Matrix (`Priority 1`, `Priority 2`, `Priority 3`)
   - `2-06/030.30 – 070.00`: Supervisory, Mutual Aid, Patrol Conduct, Shift Operations & Performance Standard
